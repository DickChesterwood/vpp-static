VirtualPairProgrammers has Closed - Frequently Asked Questions

The site will be closing on 26 September 2025.

I have an active subscription with months to run, what happens?

Firstly, we're very sorry for this inconvenience. We contacted all subscribers with details of how to access their courses, but with the vagaries of email, our message may have not reached you. Please contact Richard Chesterwood at his LinkedIn page and he will try to help.

Will there be more courses at another platform in the future?

It is possible that more courses might be released from one or both of us in future, but we can't promise this.

Will the courses stay on Udemy?

YES. The K8s and Istio courses are still maintained and supported. A new edition of each is quietly created every year, and this will continue for the foreseeable future.

I was planning to subscribe for access to archive material — is all that content lost?

Although there is a lot of value in the archive, we feel that with a lack of updates, the material is to old to realistically offer for sale.
