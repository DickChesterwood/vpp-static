Virtual Pair Programmers - 6 January 2009 - 26 September 2025

With regret, the site closed on 26 September 2025.

Virtual Pair Programmers launched its first course in 2009 — the first online course covering the Spring Framework. Over the years, we released fifty courses and were proud to welcome more than 20,000 software developers from 92 countries.

Thank you for learning with us, supporting us, and sharing this adventure. It’s been a privilege to teach you, and we’re grateful for every moment.
